A fan-made tool for managing AFK buyer services across boss runs. Track Resurrection, Time Leap, runner incidents, and revive deadlines while Next Action guides the cycle.

ⓘSee instructions
  1. Setup Party: Click . Add every available Res and TL, assign parties, and mark the Sed Bishop. Extra Res is kept as backup; any ready TL may be used.
  2. Start Run: Click when the service begins. This records the start without beginning a death timer.
  3. Buyer Death: Whenever the buyer dies, click to begin the 9-minute revive window.
  4. Follow the accordingly.
  5. Revive: Pending deaths and the matching Res buttons share a colour. After the cast, click the exact ; everyone named on it is revived in one action.
  6. Time Leap: When any Bucc uses TL, click . It resets every living, connected Res user. Main and extra TL are interchangeable.
  7. Deaths/DC: Record every death—even when several people are down. Use to review exact revive groups. A dead Bishop or Bucc is a runner and becomes an exact Res target automatically.
  8. Body Part Dies (optional): When a HT dies, click it to record the event.
  9. Export Log: Click to save the event log for runner deaths, disconnects, and splits.
  10. Correct missed actions: Click for an unrecorded Res or TL, a wrong party setup, or to mark someone revived without consuming a tracked skill. Other timers keep running.
  11. Undo a mistake: Click , or press Ctrl+Z, to reverse the latest recorded action. Active timers keep counting from their original timestamps.
